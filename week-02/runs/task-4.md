### You

You are a retail analyst for Wildframe Coffee Roasters, a specialty coffee roaster that sells beans online and through a monthly subscription. Our customers are environmentally conscious urban professionals. We can run one four-week pop-up stand this spring, with a total rent budget of $5,000 for the four weeks.

Weekly figures from comparable past events:
- Riverside Farmers Market: 9,000 visitors per week; 2% of visitors buy; average sale $14 (mostly whole-bean bags); rent $900 per week.
- Union Station concourse: 30,000 visitors per week; 0.5% of visitors buy; average sale $6 (mostly single cups); rent $2,000 per week.
- Campus library atrium: 6,000 visitors per week; 3% of visitors buy; average sale $5 (single cups); rent $600 per week.

Work through this step by step before recommending, showing your arithmetic:
1. For each location, calculate weekly buyers and weekly sales revenue.
2. Calculate each location's total rent for four weeks and check it against the $5,000 budget.
3. Calculate each location's four-week sales revenue minus four-week rent.
4. Judge how well each location fits our business and customers: whether its typical sale (whole-bean bags vs. single cups) suits a roaster that sells beans online and by subscription, and whether its visitors are likely to match our customer base.
5. Weigh these factors against each other.

Use only the figures given. If you need an assumption, state it explicitly. Then give the single biggest risk of your choice in one sentence. End your response with a single line that starts with Recommendation: followed by the location's name, and nothing after that line.

### Claude

# Step 1: Weekly Buyers and Revenue

**Riverside Farmers Market**
- Buyers: 9,000 × 2% = 180/week
- Revenue: 180 × $14 = $2,520/week

**Union Station Concourse**
- Buyers: 30,000 × 0.5% = 150/week
- Revenue: 150 × $6 = $900/week

**Campus Library Atrium**
- Buyers: 6,000 × 3% = 180/week
- Revenue: 180 × $5 = $900/week

# Step 2: Four-Week Rent vs. $5,000 Budget

| Location | Weekly Rent | 4-Week Rent | Within Budget? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Riverside | $900 | $3,600 | Yes (leaves $1,400) |
| Union Station | $2,000 | $8,000 | **No — exceeds by $3,000** |
| Campus Library | $600 | $2,400 | Yes (leaves $2,600) |

**Assumption:** Since only one four-week stand can be run, the full four weeks of rent must be paid at whichever location is chosen. Union Station is therefore not affordable at all under this budget — it's disqualified regardless of revenue potential.

# Step 3: Four-Week Profit (Revenue − Rent)

- **Riverside:** (180×4×$14) − $3,600 = $10,080 − $3,600 = **$6,480**
- **Union Station:** (150×4×$6) − $8,000 = $3,600 − $8,000 = **−$4,400** (and infeasible anyway)
- **Campus Library:** (180×4×$5) − $2,400 = $3,600 − $2,400 = **$1,200**

# Step 4: Strategic Fit

- **Riverside:** Sales are "mostly whole-bean bags," directly reinforcing the core online/subscription bean business. Farmers-market shoppers skew toward quality- and sustainability-conscious buyers — a strong match for "environmentally conscious urban professionals."
- **Union Station:** Single-cup commuter sales don't build bean/subscription habits, and the audience is grabbing convenience coffee, not necessarily evaluating a specialty roaster. Also over budget.
- **Campus Library:** Single-cup sales again don't showcase the bean/subscription product. Audience is students, not urban professionals — a weaker demographic match, and lowest profit of the feasible options.

# Step 5: Weighing the Factors

Riverside dominates on every axis: it's the only location that is both financially strongest (highest profit, $6,480) and the best strategic fit (whole-bean format, environmentally-minded audience). Campus Library is feasible financially but weak on both profit and audience/product fit. Union Station fails the hard budget constraint outright, so it's eliminated regardless of its other merits.

**Biggest risk:** As an outdoor seasonal market, Riverside's foot traffic and sales are more exposed to weather disruptions than the indoor venues, which could reduce actual results below these estimates.

Recommendation: Riverside Farmers Market
