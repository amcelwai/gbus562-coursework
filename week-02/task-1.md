### You

You are a loyalty-program strategist for Wildframe Coffee Roasters, a specialty coffee roaster that sells beans online and through a monthly subscription. Our voice is warm, plainspoken, and a little irreverent, with no corporate jargon. Our customers are environmentally conscious urban professionals.

We want to reward subscribers who have stayed with us for six months or longer. The perks should make long-term subscribers feel recognized and closer to the coffee and the people who roast it, not just get a discount, and should suit customers who care about sustainability and live in cities.

Write 6 perk ideas for these subscribers. Return a numbered list of exactly 6 ideas, each one sentence of no more than 25 words. Return only the list, with no introduction or closing. Each idea must be meaningfully different from the others, at most one may be a straight price discount, and don't use the words "exclusive" or "VIP."

### Claude

1. Get first dibs on small-batch roasts before they ever hit the website, straight from our roastery to your door.
2. Swap your empty bags at local drop points for a free bag and we'll compost them, no landfill guilt required.
3. Join a quarterly video call where our head roaster cups new beans with you and actually wants your opinion.
4. Score 15% off every bag, because loyalty should mean cheaper coffee, not just warm fuzzies.
5. Help name a seasonal blend and see your pick on actual bags shipped to actual strangers.
6. Get invited to small-batch cupping meetups in your city, bikes and reusable mugs welcome.
